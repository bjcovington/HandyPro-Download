Privacy Policy

HandyPro is designed as a desktop-first business operations tool. This website does not require account creation and does not collect customer data through forms by default.

Downloads

Installer downloads are served through GitHub Releases. GitHub may process standard request data according to GitHub’s own policies.

Application data

Business records entered into HandyPro are handled by the installed desktop application and its configured storage behavior, not by this static website.

Contact

For questions, use the support or repository contact method you provide with your HandyPro distribution.
